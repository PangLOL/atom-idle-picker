Idle trigger

These 8 frames play once after 3.5 seconds of standing, then idle resumes. Click Preview to watch that cycle at 15 fps.

Hockey stop

Second row, frames 1–3 from the Amy sheet, flipped to face right. Suggested trigger: brk is already on CAN id 0x17C bit 0 of byte 4. Play this once when the brake is true and deceleration is at least 10 km/h per second (about 0.28 g) for 150–200 ms, and speed is still above about 25 km/h. That is a firm press, not a panic stop. If it fires too often, raise it to 12–15 km/h/s. Drop out when the brake is released or deceleration falls below 6 km/h/s.
